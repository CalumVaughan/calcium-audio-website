PluginShootout Audio V1

Help & support.

More listening. Less switching plugins on and off.

PluginShootout lets you audition a pool of audio effects one at a time, move your favourites to the front and delete the rest. Survival of the fittest for your effects chain.

Originally built for comparing amp simulations, it works with any audio effects—from finding a sound you weren’t looking for to listening closely to the differences between two compressors.

01

Start with a pool.

Place PluginShootout before your comparison effects in the same Audio Effect Rack chain. Play your source audio, then use Previous and Next to listen through the candidates.

PS→FX 1FX 2FX 3

Each instance controls the devices to its right, up to the next PluginShootout or the end of that track or rack chain.

02

The controls.

Previous / Next
Switch between candidates. The selected effect is enabled; the other comparison effects are disabled.
Move to Front
Move a favourite to the front of the comparison order, then carry on auditioning.
Delete
Remove the selected candidate. At a parent level, a rack counts as one candidate, so deleting it removes the whole rack.
Popup
Keep the comparison controls in a floating window. Drag the hand to move it; click X to close. Its instance number matches the rack device.
Options
Manage plugin windows, enable Auto-Reveal GUIs, change colours and popup size, collapse devices, or open controller mapping.
MIDI / Key Mapping
Open Options → Mapping, then use Live’s MIDI or Key Map to assign Previous, Next, Move to Front and Delete.

Mapped actions do not support Undo/Redo. Use the ordinary mouse buttons when you need normal Undo/Redo.

03

From effects to whole chains.

One pool

Compare individual effects or different presets of the same effect. The workflow applies to any audio effect, not just amp simulations.

Several pools

Use multiple instances on one track, each with its own candidates. Make an independent choice in each pool to build a combined sound. To compare several effects together as one candidate, put them inside a rack.

Nests inside nests

A parent instance can choose between ordinary devices and racks containing their own PluginShootout. Each child handles the candidates inside its own chain. Repeat this inside deeper racks to compare whole approaches, then refine the choices within them without rebuilding your setup.

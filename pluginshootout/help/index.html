Plugin Shootout

Compare audio effects in Ableton Live.

Got a rack full of effects to try? Plugin Shootout switches between them while you listen. Move your favourites to the front and delete the ones you don’t want to keep.

I originally built it to compare amp sims. It works with any audio effects: explore different sounds, compare presets or listen closely to the differences between plugins.

  • Compare individual effects or complete chains.
  • Use nested racks to audition choices within a chain.
  • Control the comparison from a popup, MIDI controller or keyboard.

01

Setup

Place Plugin Shootout before your comparison effects in the same Audio Effect Rack chain. Play your source audio, then use Previous and Next to listen through the candidates.

PS→FX 1FX 2FX 3

Each instance controls the devices to its right, up to the next Plugin Shootout or the end of that track or rack chain.

02

Controls

Previous / Next
Switch between candidates. The selected effect is enabled; the other comparison effects are disabled.
Move to Front
Move a favourite to the front of the comparison order, then carry on auditioning.
Delete
Remove the selected candidate. At a parent level, a rack counts as one candidate, so deleting it removes the whole rack.
Popup
Keep the comparison controls in a floating window. Drag the hand to move it; click X to close. Its instance number matches the rack device.
Options
Manage plugin windows, enable Auto-Reveal GUIs, change colours and popup size, collapse devices, or open controller mapping.
MIDI / Key Mapping
Open Options → Mapping, then use Live’s MIDI or Key Map to assign Previous, Next, Move to Front and Delete.

Mapped actions do not support Undo/Redo. Use the ordinary mouse buttons when you need normal Undo/Redo.

03

Racks & nesting

One pool

Compare individual effects or different presets of the same effect. The workflow applies to any audio effect, not just amp simulations.

Several pools

Use multiple instances on one track, each with its own candidates. Make an independent choice in each pool to build a combined sound. To compare several effects together as one candidate, put them inside a rack.

Nests inside nests

A parent instance can choose between ordinary devices and racks containing their own Plugin Shootout. Each child handles the candidates inside its own chain. You can keep nesting: choose a rack at the top level, then work your way into it and compare the effects inside. Useful when you like a whole chain but still want to try a different part of it.
